Privacy Policy for ShowTap

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ShowTap – Live Soundboard ("ShowTap", the "App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited information to understand how the App is used and to improve its features, reliability, and user experience.

  • Usage and Product Interaction Data: We may collect information about how you interact with the App, such as app launches, feature usage, and interactions with soundboard features. This information is used for analytics and product improvement.
  • Device and Analytics Information: We may collect limited technical information associated with your device or App installation, such as device identifiers, device type, operating system version, App version, language, and similar technical information used for analytics.

We do not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

We do not collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address through the App.

2. Your Audio Files and Soundboards

ShowTap allows you to import your own audio files and organize them into soundboards.

Audio files that you import into ShowTap are stored locally on your device for use by the App. We do not upload your imported audio files to our servers, and we do not access, listen to, analyze, or collect the contents of those audio files.

Soundboard configurations and related data may also be stored locally on your device. Unless a feature specifically informs you otherwise, this data is not uploaded to our servers.

If you choose to export or share a soundboard or audio file using features provided by your device, the destination and handling of that data are controlled by you and the service you choose to use.

3. Analytics

ShowTap may use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to help us understand how users interact with the App.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as device or App instance identifiers, App interactions, session information, device information, and other usage information.

We use this information to understand App usage, evaluate existing features, identify areas for improvement, and plan future features. We do not use Firebase Analytics data for third-party advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ShowTap may offer in-app purchases and auto-renewing subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment account information. Apple handles payment processing according to its own terms and privacy policies.

The App may receive information from Apple that is necessary to determine the status of your purchases or subscriptions and to provide access to purchased features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Limited analytics information may be processed by service providers such as Google Firebase as described above. We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention

Audio files and soundboard data stored locally in ShowTap remain on your device until you remove them or delete the App, subject to the storage and backup behavior of your device and operating system.

Analytics information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their applicable data retention policies and the configuration of those services.

7. Children's Privacy

ShowTap is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
